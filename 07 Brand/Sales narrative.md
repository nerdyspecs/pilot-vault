## Hero
# No job goes unseen.
Knot puts every job in your workshop on every phone — yours, your team's, and your customer's.

**[Become a founding workshop]**

## The problem (the manifesto)
Your workshop runs on a whiteboard and your memory.

Every job in the shop lives in your head. Who's on the Myvi. What the Hilux is waiting for.
Whether anyone called the customer back about the gearbox. When you're standing on the floor,
it works. When you're not — nothing moves, and nobody notices.

A job stalls waiting on a part. The mechanic thinks the front desk knows. The front desk
thinks the mechanic is still on it. Three days later the customer calls, and that call is how
you find out. *"I told him already"* — but nobody can say when, or to whom.

The whiteboard can't follow you home. It can't tell the customer anything. It can't remember
what happened last month. And it was never the whiteboard doing the work — it was you,
holding the whole shop in your head.

## What Knot is
One board. Every job on it. On every phone in the shop — the counter, the floor, and yours,
wherever you are. When a job moves, everyone sees it move. When a job stops, you see that
too — *before* the customer calls, not after.

## How it works (three beats)
1. **Every job, one board.** A car comes in, the job goes up. Stage by stage — registered,
   assigned, in repair, ready — visible to the whole team, from anywhere.
2. **Every handoff, confirmed.** Work passes from front desk to foreman to mechanic to
   warehouse with a confirmation, not a shout across the floor. Nothing sits in between with
   everyone assuming someone else has it.
3. **Nothing waits quietly.** A job stuck on a part or a customer callback is marked
   blocked — on the board, in the open, until someone clears it.

## The kicker
And your customer? They get a link. They watch their own car move through the shop — no app
to install, no account, no calling the front desk for the third time. The calls stop, and
what replaces them is trust.

## Objections
- *"My mechanics won't use an app."* — Updating Knot is a tap, faster than walking to the
  board. Plain words, no icons to decipher, on the phone already in their pocket.
- *"The whiteboard works fine."* — It does, while you're looking at it. Knot is for every
  hour you're not.
- *"My shop is too small for software."* — Knot is built for small workshops, not chains.
  One honest fee for the whole shop — never per person, so putting your whole crew on costs
  nothing extra.

## The offer
Knot is being built now, with a small number of real workshops — not sold off a shelf.
Founding workshops run their real jobs on it, tell us where it's wrong, and shape what it
becomes. In return: a direct line to the builder, and the founding price, locked, for good.

If your shop runs on a whiteboard and your memory, we want you.
