The problem

Your workshop runs on a whiteboard and your memory.

Every job in the shop lives in your head. Who's on the Myvi. What the Hilux is waiting for. Whether anyone called the customer back about the gearbox. When you're standing on the floor, it works. When you're not — nothing moves, and nobody notices.

A job stalls waiting on a part. The mechanic thinks the front desk knows. The front desk thinks the mechanic is still on it. Three days later the customer calls, and that call is how you find out. "I told him already" — but nobody can say when, or to whom.

The whiteboard can't follow you home. It can't tell the customer anything. It was never the whiteboard doing the work — it was you, holding the whole shop in your head.

What Knot is

One board. Every job on it. On every phone in the shop.

The counter, the floor, and yours — wherever you are. When a job moves, everyone sees it move. When a job stops, you see that too. Before the customer calls, not after.

For your customers

They get a link. The calls stop.

Your customer watches their own car move through the shop — no app to install, no account, no calling the front desk for the third time. What replaces the calls is trust.
