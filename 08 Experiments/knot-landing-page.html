The board

Every job, every handoff, visible to the whole team.

The counter sees what the floor sees. The floor sees what the warehouse sees. When a job passes from one person to the next, the handoff is on the board — who has it, since when, and what it's waiting on.

A job that stalls turns amber on its own. You see it from across the room, before anyone complains. The shop stops living in one person's head.
