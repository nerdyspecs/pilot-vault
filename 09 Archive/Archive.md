# Archive
Closed material that no longer needs reading each session — moved here to keep the live
docs focused on what's current. Nothing here is deleted or rewritten; it's relocated as-is.

## What belongs here
- **Closed sprint sections** from [[Sprint plan]] once ✅ closed/built and their exit
  criteria are met — the task checklist itself stops being load-bearing once the summary
  lives in the worklog and any real decisions are captured as ADRs.
- **Old worklog sessions** once they're several sprints back and no longer "recent context"
  for picking up work.

## What never comes here
- **ADRs** — permanent, binding, superseded only by dated footnotes in place. Archiving
  would break "supersede in place, never delete the reasoning history."
- **Deferred design / Open questions / Product gaps / Risk ledger** — these are open, not
  closed; they need reviewing at their stated trigger points, which is the opposite of this
  folder's purpose.
- **Rejected alternatives** — kept live on purpose, so a dead end isn't re-proposed.
- **Concepts notes** (Data model, Event log, etc.) — living reference docs describing the
  current shape of the system; they're updated in place, not sprint-scoped history.
